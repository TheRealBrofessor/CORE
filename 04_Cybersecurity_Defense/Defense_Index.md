# Cybersecurity Defense Index

This area is CORE's defensive-security core: thinking about threats, hardening systems, logging and monitoring, responding to incidents, basic forensics awareness, and reusable blue-team playbooks. Nothing here covers offensive techniques — see [[CORE_Principles]] for why.

## Notes In This Area

- [[Threat_Modeling]] — thinking systematically about what could go wrong and why.
- [[Hardening]] — reducing attack surface on systems you control.
- [[Logging_Monitoring]] — seeing what's happening on your systems.
- [[Incident_Response]] — what to do when something has actually gone wrong.
- [[Forensics_Basics]] — preserving and understanding evidence after an event, at a conceptual level.
- [[Blue_Team_Playbooks]] — how the playbooks in `09_Playbooks` fit together.
